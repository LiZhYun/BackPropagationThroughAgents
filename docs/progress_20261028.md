## Final-policy diagnostic and next controlled comparison

The [corrected diagnostic](../experiments/hc_agentmixer_final_diagnostic_complete_20261028.json) exactly reproduces all nine AgentMixer 10M endpoints and every raw record in their original 90 evaluation episodes. It checks 90,000 additional evaluation decisions and 540,000 scalar action coordinates, without changing training artifacts. An earlier diagnostic assertion incorrectly required a float32 log/exp round trip to be bit-identical; its failure and three partial replay files are retained. The correction relaxes only that standard-deviation check, not exact actions, means or raw episode replay.

Residual central standard deviations reach their numerical upper limit on 12.96–20.64% of evaluation action coordinates. Identity seed 2 also reaches it on 4.18%. Joint numerical floors are inactive on these trajectories. Mean-action clipping is common across learned and identity controls, so these measurements alone do not explain the performance gap. Training-distribution entropy and evaluation-trajectory scale measurements describe different states.

The [next frozen comparison](../experiments/hc2x3_entropy0_10m_plan_20261028.json) changes only the entropy coefficient from 0.01 to 0, retaining the same 20-module source, three variants, seeds 0–2, 10M budget and evaluation grid. Zero matches the audited MuJoCo release coefficient. All nine new and nine prior outcomes must validate before the paired comparison; no gain is assumed. Any selected configuration still needs fresh, disjoint final training seeds.
