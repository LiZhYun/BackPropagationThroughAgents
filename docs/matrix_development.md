# Matrix development evidence

These are one-step implementation and optimization experiments. The pinned released matrix environment also terminates after each decision and returns constant observations. Its training logger multiplies mean step reward by the rollout length, 200, while evaluation reports reward per decision. This plausibly explains BPTA's plotted scale of 2,200 versus our climbing ceiling of 11, but the historical figure-to-logger mapping is unverified. See the [curve and protocol audit](other_curve_targets.md). Exact enumeration is used only for evaluation, never for selecting training actions or computing training advantages.

The initial sweep used all four algorithms, both games, and training seeds 0, 1, 2: 100,000 sampled joint decisions, batch 1,024, four PPO epochs, entropy coefficient 0.01. All 24 processes completed, but none achieved an optimal final greedy policy. AR-PPO and BPPO selected reward 7 on climbing and 2 on penalty. AgentMixer selected the same actions while its centralized modifier sharpened distributions much more than the local policies. Every run is retained in `results/matrix_dev_20261024T222903Z` (job 21192344).

A numerical test found float32 mean rounding could normalize a constant advantage vector to a nonzero constant. Moment accumulation now uses float64, and a constant vector becomes exactly zero. The otherwise identical frozen rerun is `results/matrix_normfix_20261024T224628Z` (job 21192446). Its completed non-AgentMixer outcomes remain suboptimal: this bug was real, but is not an adequate explanation for the exploration failure.

Frozen-checkpoint diagnostics show an important difficulty. Near the final BPPO policies, optimal joint actions have probabilities around 4e-6–2e-5 on climbing and 4e-9–9e-9 on penalty. These policies almost never revisit useful branches. Full conditional successor-ratio feedback vanishes when new and old successor policies agree. Treating the denominator as a buffered scalar gives a different derivative; that alternative is not silently substituted and would require a named ablation.

The initial control settings also differ from the BPTA matrix appendix: it lists one million steps, 50 rollout threads, an episode-length setting of 200, 15 PPO epochs, a 64-unit ReLU hidden layer, and gradient clipping at 10. In the pinned code, that setting controls rollout length rather than environment termination. The source identifies high-dimensional predecessor-action projection as useful, without fully specifying the projection. The current two-layer tanh network and direct one-hot predecessor inputs are explicitly development controls.

Next comparisons use matched AR-PPO/BPPO learned predecessor projections and entropy schedules. AgentMixer additionally tests smooth log-scale bounds, `b*tanh(raw/b)` with `b=log(2)`, limiting gap scales to [0.5,2] while preserving initial identity and local modes. Changes are recorded in configurations and frozen source snapshots. Improvements will be evaluated across all development seeds before freezing independent final seeds. Neither more tuning nor a successful smoke test establishes the papers' performance claims.

The first entropy schedule (0.1 held for half the budget, then decayed to 0.01) completed in job21192554. BPPO reached the penalty optimum in 2/3 seeds, AR-PPO in 1/3; neither reached the climbing optimum. MAPPO and bounded-scale factorized AgentMixer reached neither optimum. The latter's local expected climbing reward improved to6.92 from1.17, while its greedy reward stayed7: avoiding a sharply concentrated centralized distribution helped local training but did not solve coordination. These three development seeds do not establish an algorithm ranking.

The [experimental joint modifier](agentmixer_joint.md) is tested separately with a matched faster-modifier factorized control. It preserves the same local greedy execution rule, but changes the centralized distribution from a product to an exactly normalized joint distribution. It has independent invariant tests and is recorded under `experimental_joint_gap_scaling`; it does not inherit the publication's convergence claims.

Both projected-input controls subsequently completed: jobs21192609 and21192611, with initial entropy coefficients0.1 and0.3 respectively. AR-PPO and BPPO reached the greedy optimum on both games in all three seeds, within100k decisions. This validates an effective shared architecture/exploration setting; it does not establish an advantage of peer feedback, since the matched AR-PPO baseline also succeeded. Learning curves and independent seeds remain necessary for a sample-efficiency or reliability claim.
